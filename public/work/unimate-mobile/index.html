The problem

Students keep their semester in five places: a calendar, a notes app, a grades spreadsheet, a timer, and a chat with an AI that knows nothing about their courses.

UniMate puts it in one app that works offline.

What I built

  • Coursework planning: colour-coded courses, five task types with priorities, attachments, due dates and times, and recurring tasks.
  • An agenda and a timetable: every task grouped by day with quick filters, and today’s classes on the dashboard.
  • Reminders that survive reboots, re-armed automatically after restarts.
  • Grades and GPA: weighted results, a live average, a letter grade and an estimated 4.0 GPA per course.
  • A focus timer and statistics: study minutes banked per course, a 7-day chart and a streak.
  • An AI study assistant that sees your real courses, tasks and schedule, plans your week as tasks you approve with one tap, and reads images and PDFs.

How it works

Flutter with Material 3, and Provider for state. Everything lives on the device in SQLite with versioned migrations; the only thing that ever leaves the phone is what you send to the Gemini assistant.

lib/db/         schema, migrations, one module per table
providers/  session, settings, data-refresh bus, Gemini
services/   notifications, backup, AI task parser
screens/    dashboard, agenda, timetable, focus, stats, AI

// SQLite schema v5 · every migration path tested

Key decisions

  • Storage modules, not an ORM: one small, testable module per table, with every read scoped to the signed-in account.
  • A data-refresh bus: one revision counter that screens watch, so there are no refresh buttons.
  • Defensive AI parsing: suggested tasks are validated field by field before anything touches the database.
  • Safe upgrades: each migration checks the live schema first, so upgrading from any older version works.
  • Your data stays yours: local accounts with salted SHA-256 hashes, per-account separation and a one-tap JSON backup.
